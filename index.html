How to play

👆Drag anywhere left or right to steer the roll.
💩Poop shrinks your roll. Four hits and you're cooked.
☣️Toxic puddles cause Brainrot: controls reverse and speed doubles for 2.5 seconds.
⚡Lightning / 🗿 regrows your roll, gives a 3-second shield and +100 Aura.
🚽Roll into a cyan IN toilet to warp forward to the pink OUT toilet.
✨Skim past hazards for close calls. Chain them to build a combo up to ×5 on all Aura.

Frequently asked questions

How is my score (Aura) calculated?

You earn 10 Aura every second you survive, +25 for each close call, +50 for each warp and +100 for each power-up. All of it is multiplied by your current combo (up to ×5). Taking any hit resets the combo.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button in the top-left corner. The game also pauses automatically when you get a call, open Control Center or leave the app, and only resumes when you tap the screen.

The pink toilet didn't warp me — is that a bug?

No. Only cyan IN toilets warp you, and always forward. Pink OUT toilets are exits, so rolling over one is harmless.

Does the game work with VoiceOver and other accessibility settings?

Yes. VoiceOver reads your score, roll size and game events, and lets you drag to steer directly. A two-finger double tap (Magic Tap) pauses. With Reduce Motion on, screen shake, pulses and drifting text are turned off. All text meets WCAG AA contrast.

Does Sigma Towel need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Do you collect any personal data?

No. Your best score is stored only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my best score?

Delete the app and reinstall it. Your best score is stored only on this device, so deleting the app erases it.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The game plays in portrait orientation.

The game feels slow or stutters. What can I do?

Close other apps, turn off Low Power Mode and make sure iOS is up to date. If it continues, email us your iPhone model and iOS version.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We'd love to hear it. Email us, and add a screenshot if you can.
