PRIVACY POLICY
Support & FAQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

The short version

Sigma Towel: Skibidi Run does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network connections and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The app saves one number, your best score, in the app's local storage on your iPhone so it can show it next time you play. It never leaves your device. It is erased when you delete the app. It may be included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple controls under its own privacy policy.

Tracking and third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any other protected information.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. To remove the locally stored best score, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
